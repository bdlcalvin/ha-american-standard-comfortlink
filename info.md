# American Standard ComfortLink

Monitor and control your **American Standard ComfortLink** Wi-Fi heat pump water
heater from Home Assistant.

**Features**
- Water heater entity: on/off, target temperature, operating mode
  (Eco / Comfort / Fast / i-Memory)
- Current water temperature & comfort setpoint sensors
- Heat pump state sensor (off / standby / heating / anti-legionella)
- Boost (resistance heating) switch

**Setup:** add the integration with your ComfortLink account email + password and
your heater's Gateway ID (its Wi-Fi MAC, e.g. `AABBCCDDEEFF`).

> Unofficial — not affiliated with American Standard, Ariston, or HTP. Cloud-based
> (no local control). See the README for full details.
